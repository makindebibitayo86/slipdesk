SlipDeskOyo State2026 Compulsory Exam

Slip Desk is an IT support tool from the Office of the Head of Service, Oyo State Government Secretariat, to help colleagues deal with issues on site during the 2026 Compulsory Examination. Load the candidates’ register to look up a candidate, check payment and print their slip, or to analyse the registration data. The activity log records who signed in, what they searched and what was printed.
