Before you begin

Follow these steps each time you use the desk.

  1. Sign in. Type your own password. The desk recognises you and records your activity under your name. Never share it.
  2. Load the register. Use the gold Load register button at the top of the screen. Check that the record count matches the latest approved file.
  3. Find the candidate. Type a name, payment reference, exam number or phone number, then pick the person from the list.
  4. Check payment. Confirmed payments open the Exam Slip. Pending ones open the Registration Slip, and the exam slip stays greyed out.
  5. Print, then step away safely. Never issue a greyed-out slip. Hand it only to the candidate, and never alter it. Press Clear so no candidate details stay on screen. The desk also locks after 5 minutes idle.
Candidate records are personal data. Do not photograph, copy or forward them. Report any lost device or misdirected printout to your supervising officer immediately.

Change your password

You are signing in for the first time with a temporary password. Change it to your own now. It takes less than a minute.

  1. Make it your own. Use at least 8 characters. Only you should know it.
  2. Type it twice. Enter it in both boxes. The two must match. Use the eye icon to check what you typed.
  3. Make it different. It cannot be the temporary password you were given, or the password of any other officer on this computer.
  4. Keep it private. Never share it or write it where others can see it. Your activity is recorded under your name.
  5. Use the same computer. Your password is saved on this computer only, so sign in here with it from now on.
If you see a Skip for now link, you may keep the temporary password for the moment. Changing to your own password is strongly advised, so that only you can sign in under your name.
